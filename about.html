About Mulberry Institute

Creating excellence in hospitality through practical education and global standards.

Who We Are

Mulberry Institute of Hospitality, founded in 2020, is a specialized culinary and hospitality training institute based in Pokhara, Nepal.

We are committed to developing skilled professionals through hands-on training, experienced instructors, and internationally recognized qualifications. Our graduates are successfully working in hotels and restaurants across Dubai, Australia, Japan, and many other countries.

Our Mission

To develop future culinary leaders by providing top-tier education, emphasizing practical experience, and maintaining the highest standards of quality and innovation in hospitality training.

Our Vision

To become a leading hospitality education institute in Nepal that produces globally competitive chefs and hospitality professionals ready for international careers.
